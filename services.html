Our services

Petroleum licensing and compliance is our core service. We also offer environmental consultancy, engineering and construction, fuel sales at our own filling station, and Dangote Cement supply.

Petroleum Licensing & Compliance

This is our main service. We guide filling-station owners through the licensing process, carry out the technical tests ourselves, and prepare the documents NMDPRA requires.

New Station Assistance

Application guidance and support for setting up a new filling station.

Licence Renewals

Renewing an existing station's licence before it lapses.
