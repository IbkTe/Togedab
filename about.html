Our story

Established in 2011, Togedab Engineering & Construction Limited helps filling-station owners meet NMDPRA licensing and compliance requirements. We carry out the leak and pressure tests and prepare the documents needed for approval. We also operate our own filling station, supply Dangote Cement for retail and distribution, and provide environmental services consultancy.
