How we get your station licensed

Every station follows the same regulated route under the Petroleum Industry Act 2021. We handle each stage for you: the tests, the paperwork and the NMDPRA submissions.

  1. 01

    Site & suitability

    We assess the location against NMDPRA siting rules and prepare the new-station application.

    Testing & certification
